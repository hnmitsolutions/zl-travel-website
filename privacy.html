Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

ZL Travel Agency (“we,” “us,” or “our”), based in Ankeny, Iowa, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect on zltravelagency.com and related pages, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a contact or quote request form
  • Use our homepage lead popup
  • Request an appointment or consultation
  • Chat with us through our website chat widget
  • Email, call, or otherwise contact us

This may include your name, email address, phone number, travel preferences, trip dates, message content, and similar details needed to help plan your trip.

We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URL, and marketing attribution data (for example UTM parameters) when present in the page URL.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide travel planning services
  • Schedule consultations and follow up about trip options
  • Operate and improve our website and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

Phone numbers and email addresses submitted on our forms are used to contact you about your travel inquiry. Submitting a form does not enroll you in SMS marketing programs, does not constitute consent for automated text messages, and does not include one-time passcode (OTP) verification.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not buy or sell leads.
  • We do not operate an affiliate lead-generation marketplace on this website.
  • We do not require SMS, text-message, or OTP consent to submit website forms.

4. Chat widget

Our website includes a chat widget provided by our customer communications platform (LeadConnector / GoHighLevel). Messages and contact details you share in chat may be stored in our CRM so we can respond to you. Use of the chat widget is optional.

5. Service providers

We use trusted service providers to help operate our business, such as website hosting, customer relationship management (CRM), and communications tools. These providers process information only as needed to provide services to us and are expected to protect it appropriately.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our site and third-party tools (including chat and analytics, if enabled) may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, and meeting legal requirements.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
