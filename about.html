About us

I know how overwhelming it can feel to plan a trip that keeps everyone happy.

With so many destinations, opinions, and moving parts to consider, it's easy to feel stuck before you even begin. That's exactly why I started ZL Travel Agency.

Before launching this business, I was the one family and friends always called when it was time to plan a vacation. I loved helping them find the perfect spot, figure out what to do, and build something that brought everyone together. After years of doing this for others, I realised it was more than a hobby — it was a way to serve families who just wanted a break, not more stress.

ZL Travel Agency is named after my twin daughters and built around that same idea: family time should feel easy, meaningful, and fun.

Today, I help families plan vacations that match their needs, interests, and pace. Whether you're dreaming of a European adventure, a cruise getaway, or an all-inclusive trip where everyone can relax, I'm here to guide you every step of the way. When you work with me, you get more than a vacation — you get support, care, and a plan that gives your family the time together you've been craving.
